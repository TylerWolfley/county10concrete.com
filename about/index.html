Founded by Jude Eppler and Tyler Wolfley

Jude Eppler and Tyler Wolfley are the founders and primary contacts for County 10 Concrete. The company is built around a professional concrete crew model, with the right people, equipment, and schedule matched to each job.

When you call, text, or send the quote form, the goal is to collect the real project details, review the scope clearly, and give you the right next step. Photos, measurements, access notes, drainage, finish type, old concrete, and schedule all matter.

County 10 Concrete is licensed and insured, offers free estimates, and keeps communication practical from the first message through the job.
