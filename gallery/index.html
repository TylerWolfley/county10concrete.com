What to Send for a Concrete Quote

Photos make the quote conversation faster. Send enough context for the team to review the work area, access, old concrete, drainage, and rough size.

  • Wide photos showing the whole driveway, patio, sidewalk, slab area, or repair spot.
  • Close photos of cracks, settling, edges, drainage issues, steps, and tie-ins.
  • Rough measurements, address, preferred finish, and whether old concrete needs removed.
  • Access notes like tight gates, slopes, low wires, parking limits, or rural driveway conditions.
