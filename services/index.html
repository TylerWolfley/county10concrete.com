FAQ

How is pricing determined?

Square footage matters, but so do prep, thickness, reinforcement, finish, access, removal, drainage, and schedule. Send photos for a fast first look.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. County 10 Concrete offers free estimates for concrete projects in the service area.

Can you replace old concrete?

Yes, project dependent. Send photos of the existing concrete so we can review tear-out, access, and replacement needs.

Do you do stamped concrete?

Yes. Stamped and decorative concrete can be reviewed for patios, walkways, entries, and outdoor spaces.

What should I send for a quote?

Send the address, city, photos from several angles, rough measurements, desired finish, access notes, and whether old concrete needs removed.
